Privacy

Carflow Puzzle is a single-player game by Funvalvar. We do not collect or upload your gameplay data.

Local game data

Your progress, completed levels, saved puzzle state, and language/sound preferences are stored on your device. We do not receive them. There is no game account, game server, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, tracking, or hosted gameplay logs.

Apple purchases

Apple processes in-app payments and purchase restoration through StoreKit. The app checks Apple-verified transactions to unlock levels 13–60. We do not receive your payment card details or operate a purchase receipt server. Apple handles transaction information under its own privacy policy.

If you email us

If you choose to contact funvalvar@proton.me, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use them to answer your support request. Email is optional and is not required to play. Do not include passwords or payment details.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until removed through device storage controls, including uninstalling the app. This may erase your progress and does not cancel an Apple purchase. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. Email us to request deletion of correspondence.

This website

This static site has no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, account system, contact form, or data upload feature. Support links open your own email application.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change and show the effective date above. For privacy questions or requests, contact Funvalvar at funvalvar@proton.me.
