Support

How to play, keep your progress, and restore the full game.

How do I move a car?

Tap a car to view its full route. Tap it again, or tap Go, to move. Only the front car can leave a lane. If the next lane is full, make space first.

Can I undo or start again?

Yes. Use Undo to take back a move, or Restart to reset the current puzzle. There is no timer.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and language/sound preferences stay on your device. There is no game account or cloud sync. Removing the app or clearing its data may erase progress. Restoring a purchase restores paid access, not game progress.

What is free, and what does the purchase unlock?

Levels 1–12 are free. One non-consumable purchase permanently unlocks the current levels 13–60, a total of 48 extra levels. There is no subscription and no promise of future content. See the in-game Apple price for your region.

How do I restore a purchase?

Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open the three-dot More menu and choose Restore purchases, or use Restore purchases on the purchase screen. If nothing is found, check your Apple Account and retry. Family Sharing depends on Apple’s family settings and eligibility.

Can I play without the internet?

Yes, gameplay is offline and no gameplay data is uploaded. Purchasing and restoring use Apple services and need a connection. If paid access cannot be verified, retry the purchase check or restore; the first 12 levels remain available.

How do I change sound or language?

Open the three-dot More menu. Sound switches both music and effects. Language offers English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese without resetting the puzzle.

Still need a hand?

Email your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. A screenshot is optional. Please do not send passwords or payment details.
